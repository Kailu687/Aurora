Aurora Pocket

Your music. Nothing else.

No Bluetooth. No Wi-Fi. No subscription. No notifications. Just the songs you own, in your pocket, playing through real headphones.

Design

One sheet of black glass.

A full-face lens with a colour screen behind it, wrapped in a slim graphite frame. Album art, title, progress. Nothing to tap, nothing to swipe.

1.69″ IPS240 × 280 colour display
Black glassedge to edge, screen behind it
52 × 104 mmnarrower than a credit card
11.3 mmthin, with a 1200 mAh battery inside
Controls

Five keys.
Feel every one.

Aluminium caps on real tactile switches. Volume up and down, a gap, then previous, play and next. The play key has a dot you can find without looking. Hold it to switch off.

3.5 mm, up top.Any wired headphones or earbuds.
USB-C and microSD, below.Charge, transfer, and swap cards without tools.
Sound

Wired. On purpose.

No pairing, no compression, no lag. Your files go from the card through a proper headphone driver, built to drive 16 to 32 Ω earbuds and headphones directly.

microSDyour files→ DecoderRP2350→ BufferPCM→ EQoptional→ DAC + headphone driverTI TLV320AIC3100→ 3.5 mm
MP3 · WAV · FLACPlays what you already have. AAC (M4A) is planned.
16–32 ΩA real headphone output, not a line-out chip.
Hardware EQRuns in the audio chip itself. Off by default.

Your library

Your songs, on a card you own.

01

Swap cards any time

Pull the microSD out, put a bigger one in. The player notices and rescans. FAT32 and exFAT, designed for cards up to 512 GB.

02

Shows up as a USB drive

Plug into a computer, or a phone that supports USB drives, and drag music across. No app, no sync software.

03

Album art from your files

Reads tags and embedded covers, and keeps small thumbnails on the card so scrolling stays quick.

04

Works with Aurora

Playlists are plain files on the card. The plan is for the Aurora app to send its playlists and favourites straight to it.

05

Updates you can’t brick

Firmware updates are a drag-and-drop file over USB-C, and recovery lives in the chip’s read-only memory.

06

Built to be opened

Four screws on the back, one custom two-layer board inside, no development boards. Made to be repaired, not replaced.

Deliberately left out

What it doesn’t do is the point.

Tech specs

V1 prototype.

Design specifications. Aurora Pocket has not been built or tested yet, so details may change. Figures marked est. come from calculations, not measurements. It is not certified for sale.

Size
52 × 104 × 11.3 mm
Finish
Matte graphite frame, full-face black lens, aluminium keys. The prototype case is 3D printed, so finish may differ from the renders
Display
1.69-inch IPS colour, 240 × 280 (ST7789V2), behind the lens
Controls
Five side keys: volume up, volume down, previous, play/pause, next. Hold play/pause for power
Processor
Raspberry Pi RP2350 microcontroller, dual Arm Cortex-M33 at 150 MHz, 520 KB RAM, 8 MB flash
Audio
3.5 mm stereo headphone jack. TI TLV320AIC3100 DAC with integrated headphone driver, for 16 to 32 Ω
Formats
MP3, WAV, FLAC. AAC-LC in M4A planned. ALAC and HE-AAC not planned
Storage
Removable microSD, FAT32 or exFAT. Designed for up to 512 GB; the largest card has not been tested yet
USB-C
Charging; appears as a USB drive (full speed, about 1 MB/s); settings over serial; drag-and-drop firmware updates
Battery
1200 mAh Li-Po with protection and temperature sensing. ~45 h continuous playback, screen offest. ~25 h screen onest.
Charging
USB-C at 500 mA, about 3 h from emptyest. Plays while charging
Wireless
None. No Bluetooth, no Wi-Fi
